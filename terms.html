Public AI Switzerland cooperative · as of July 2026 · draft for review by the governing bodies

These terms are a draft and apply subject to formal incorporation of the cooperative. The authoritative legal framework is set out in the statutes.

1. Scope

These terms govern your relationship with Public AI Switzerland when subscribing to cooperative shares and using our services, in particular Apertus.

2. The cooperative

Public AI Switzerland is a cooperative under Art. 828 ff. of the Swiss Code of Obligations. Until formal registration, contributions are held in trust.

3. Share and membership

You join by subscribing to at least two shares of CHF 50.– each (CHF 100.– total). Each member has one vote.

4. Research and development fund

From the second year of membership you contribute at least CHF 50.– per year to the research and development fund.

5. Use of Apertus

Using Apertus is separate from membership. The chat is generally free; the API is billed by usage.

6. Availability and warranty

We strive for reliable operation but cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability.

7. Liability

To the extent permitted by law, we are liable only for damages arising from gross negligence or intent.

8. Intellectual property

Apertus is an open model. The brand, logo and editorial content of Public AI Switzerland are protected.

9. Changes

We may amend these terms and inform members of material changes in good time.

10. Applicable law and jurisdiction

Swiss law applies exclusively. The place of jurisdiction is the seat of the cooperative.
